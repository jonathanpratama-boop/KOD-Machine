# Deferred proposals and decision gates

The implemented work connects timing evidence, bounded observation, prepared
context and paper operations. The proposals below remain proposals. None is a
running experiment, new schedule, live-order feature or claim of profitability.

## Primary next operating step

Run one declared-slot intraday observation from a permitted operating host after
the engineering protocol is committed. Done means retained membership, exact
request/receipt evidence, explicit completed/partial/blocked coverage and a
readable timing board. A source refusal is an observed failure, not a successful
market scan. Resolve access through supported sources and permitted environments;
do not bypass provider restrictions.

Then accumulate the protocol's thirty scheduled operating attempts. The resulting
report should separate threshold-information delay, collection/scheduler delay,
analysis delay and execution/liquidity constraints. Unknown stages remain unknown.
This is an operations diagnostic; it is not a strategy promotion sample.

## Live-money gate remains closed

Current enforcement refuses `ENTRY_ELIGIBLE` in the candidate workflow. Timing
context always returns `entry_permission: false`. There is no broker credential
store, live order adapter, leverage enablement or automatic promotion path in this
release. A paper intent is never represented as a real order.

Any future live implementation must be a separate owner-approved change. Before
that change could be considered, its design would need all of the following:

1. A named frozen strategy with a precisely defined population, information
   cutoff, decision rule, horizon, costs and failure conditions. The evidence must
   support executable net outcomes under the intended venue rather than label
   opens or peak returns. Required sample size and uncertainty follow the design;
   thirty operating attempts, sixty forecasts or an elapsed trial duration do not
   automatically meet this requirement.
2. An explicitly confirmed capital and loss budget, portfolio drawdown limit,
   exposure limits, and stress/gap assumptions. These are owner choices, not
   numbers inferred from an aspiration or whole-position preference. Structural
   planned price protection must respect the current five-percent maximum while
   acknowledging that costs and gaps can produce a larger realized loss.
3. Verified broker/product access, current fees, quote currency, account FX,
   liquidity, minimum sizes, feasible protective orders and a sustainable
   monitoring/expiry plan. A proxy quote or an alert cannot supply protection.
4. Separately tested order reconciliation, rejection/partial-fill handling,
   stale-data behavior, duplicate prevention, kill and recovery controls. Unknown
   holdings or order state must stop dependent actions.
5. Explicit owner sign-off for each trade under the selected active plan. A rank,
   prediction, conditional-ready assessment or model promotion is not permission
   to spend money. The owner must separately approve any later change to this
   authorization model.

No arbitrary maximum latency such as thirty minutes is introduced as a universal
entry rule. The feasible deadline depends on the event, expected remaining payoff,
quote quality, costs and ability to protect the position. If the opportunity is
gone or essential evidence is missing, the correct output remains NO TRADE.
