Rule: exact archived Upbit listing-title family, KRW market, already observable in Binance hourly data before the notice, entry at the first full hour after publication, hold24hours, five random contemporaneously available archive controls. The control pool comes from historical archive directories, including delisted instruments, with static product exclusions. Control eligibility is decided before exit outcomes. Event costs are deducted against a gross control benchmark so the owner’s friction hurdle is not cancelled.

The primary horizon, costs, control sampling and statistics were published in freeze commit e97a93e before this run read historical price outcomes. The original exploratory plan did not specify its primary horizon; this addendum selected24hours before execution. The original study’s full leak, mood, ignition-overlap and reaction diagnostics were not claimed complete. Secondary6/72-hour results cannot rescue the primary gate.

All source/implementation corrections and superseded results are retained. See IMPLEMENTATION_REPAIRS.md and TIMESTAMP_DIAGNOSTIC.json. The last correction accepts a final millisecond encoded in microseconds as the nominal hourly boundary while retaining the exact source timestamp, and isolates unrelated malformed rows without using them as prices. No hypothetical price was interpolated.

## Immediate decision

This rule does not qualify for promotion to a prospective opportunity strategy when the primary gate fails. More frequent scans, a larger server or waiting for the plumbing cohort do not change this historical result. The existing daily-mover screen remains a descriptive research tool, with no validated entry policy. The owner’s earlier failed early-entry studies and close rule are preserved.

Use this result to make the next research decision rather than leaving an unrun test as an indefinite dependency. The next information family or process must have its own falsifiable question and original evidence; relabelling the failed price/volume/listing rules does not create a passed strategy. The cloud collector may continue as background engineering evidence; it is not a gate on reviewing this report.

## Verification and limits

Independent arithmetic reproduced the means from serialized event rows. 498 downloaded archives match official SHA-256 checksums. Frozen addendum/exclusion/original-plan hashes match. The tests and exact source are included. Raw archives and metadata are retained in the companion evidence package.

Historical hourly opens/closes are execution proxies; no real fills occurred. The2%cost is the owner’s fixed research assumption, not a current broker fee verification; additional spread, slippage, FX and account access remain unresolved. Exact ticker matching and archive measurability can omit renamed/incomplete instruments. Notice revisions and archive corrections cannot establish historical publication. Date clustering handles same-day dependence; same-asset serial dependence across days remains. Historical periods had been seen in earlier KOD work, so this is not an untouched forward validation.
