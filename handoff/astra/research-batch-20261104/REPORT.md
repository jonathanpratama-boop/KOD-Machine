## The thesis
KOD's next potential advantage is interpreting a concrete change in who receives money or bears dilution. The hypothesis is that some such changes leave a measurable price effect after the information becomes actionable. That is unproven: public changes can already be priced, benefits can go to the treasury or liquidity providers rather than the token, and transaction costs can consume the remaining move.

The main positive-return question is H1 below. H2 and H3 are warnings to test, not substitutes for finding profitable entries. No asset is recommended for purchase by this batch.

## What the first batch taught us

The source checks ran in parallel. They are completed feasibility tests, **not three completed profitability backtests**.

1. The free governance feed works from both the Mac and the Singapore server. Its votes need further evidence before they can represent executed policy. Three inspected proposal bodies produced zero fully execution-qualified cases; this is not a claim that the whole market has zero cases. A GMX buyback policy, a Sushi emissions increase and a Balancer future redemption plan illustrate why a simple keyword signal would misclassify the economics.
2. H3's current official catalog has 446 distinct articles across nine retained pages. It begins in February 2022, so it cannot provide the promised 2020–21 source history. Under the original rule, metadata permit at most nine exact-USDT targets in development and thirteen in validation, before body/price checks; the bar is thirty targets and ten independent announcement dates in each segment.
3. A possible source-driven date amendment was considered before prices, then rejected rather than published. Its 2022–23 development segment still has at most 22 exact-USDT targets on nine dates. No test threshold was relaxed and no further date-window search was performed. Missing USDT archives are measurement gaps, not proof an asset never traded or lost money.

This stopped a backtest that could not meet its preset admission criteria. H3 remains scientifically untested under this rule; it was not declared unprofitable.

## The next concrete work package

**Build a small verified cashflow event register before writing another strategy.** Start with one complete H1 case and one H2 counterexample, using the same retained evidence structure. Each must connect:

official asset identity → old versus new policy → earliest public disclosure → actual authority/approval → successful implementation → amount/recipient/supply effect → whether a spot holder can benefit.

That is the next evidence gap to resolve. It does not depend on waiting five days. Complete those two source chains before scaling extraction; if they cannot be established, stop the lane instead of building more automation around unverifiable data. A full return study starts only when a sufficient, outcome-blind register and exact entry/exit/benchmark/cost rules are frozen. A first source example or persuasive investment narrative is not an edge.

For a concrete case, the eventual prediction must say **which asset, why value changes, by when, what price already reflects, what would disprove it, and how it compares with cash after costs**. No arbitrary probability or target is supplied merely to fill a template.

## Operating model to move faster

- Run independent acquisition, source review and code review in parallel; serialize the dependent steps of classification, rule freeze and outcome scoring.
- Reuse one data pull and the existing archive loader. Keep separate hypotheses, denominators and results. All inspected ideas and exclusions stay visible.
- Apply minimum source/sample checks before expensive price downloads. No broad parameter search, repeated threshold tuning or selecting the best-looking historical winner.
- Separate a historical screen from a fresh paper test. Future outcome time cannot be compressed by more frequent polling. Positive historical results do not authorize real trades.
- Keep the live reliability test and existing approved paper work independent. No new paid provider, recurring opportunity scanner or automated trading was deployed in this pass.
