# Pre-outcome timing clarification — batch 01

Independent review found imprecise prose in the frozen protocol/charter. The exact formula is unchanged: entry = floor(publication time to UTC hour) + 1 hour. This is a next-full-hour entry proxy with a delay greater than zero and at most 60 minutes. It is NOT a guarantee of one full hour to notice, review and act. References to a 'one-hour processing delay' in the introductory hypothesis must be read as this next-hour convention. No claim of robustness to a full 60-minute manual delay follows.

This is an additive wording correction before any H3 price outcome is read. Event eligibility, time windows, costs, controls, sample gates and formula are unchanged.
