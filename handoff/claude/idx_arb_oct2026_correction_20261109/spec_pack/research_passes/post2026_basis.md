# IDX ARB basis from 2027-01-01 (research as of 2026-11-09; web only)

Verdict: SCHEDULED_BUT_SUBJECT_TO_EVALUATION. No official text was fetched (idx.co.id / ojk.go.id not retrieved); all via press.

## Established (multiple outlets)
- Kep-00136/BEI/09-2026 (issued 2026-09-21): two phases. Phase 1 2026-09-28..2026-12-31; phase 2 from 2027-01-01 with ARB symmetric to ARA (IDXChannel 2026-09-27: "penerapan batas ARB yang simetris dengan ARA"; Katadata 2026-09-21).
- Symmetric 2027 table (Katadata, Invezgo, Ajaib, Maybank): Rp1-10 = Rp1 both; Rp11-200 = 35%/35%; >200-5000 = 25%/25%; >5000 = 20%/20%.
- BEI director Irvan Susandy (Bisnis Insight 2026-09-24, said 09-23): symmetric is "kondisi normalnya"; "kami akan lihat perkembangan terakhir sampai akhir tahun"; "sementara ini rencana yang begitu". Headline: BEI has not confirmed symmetric will run early 2027; will not force it if market not conducive.
- Kep-00157/BEI/10-2026 (effective 2026-10-19) replaced Kep-00136: ARB 7% for >Rp10 (Rp11-200 band per some), valid "sampai 31 Desember 2026", BEI "akan mengevaluasinya kembali dengan mempertimbangkan perkembangan pasar" (Journalarta, Lensa Medan, Receh). Journalarta: kept or adjusted again; no mention of 2027/symmetric.
- Kep-00158/BEI/10-2026 (2026-10-20): Rp11-14 ARB = Rp1; replaces Kep-00157; "BEI will evaluate before deciding whether to continue after 31 Dec 2026" (search summaries of Bareksa, Media Indonesia, Warta Ekonomi etc.; I did not read each page).
- OJK (reported by Kalderanews, Nov 2026; page 403 on fetch): ARB 7% runs until 2026-12-31 and will be re-evaluated; buyback-without-RUPS extended to 2027-06-30 (that 2027 date is buybacks, not ARB).
- MSCI: consultation on moving Indonesia from Emerging to Standalone 2026-10-15..2027-04-30, decision by 2027-06-30 (Rikopedia/CNBC). Methodology weight change Feb 2027 (Bisnis). Nothing found tying MSCI to a change of the 2027 ARB.

## Not found
- No report up to 2026-11-09 of any extension of 7% beyond 2026-12-31, nor any BEI/OJK statement confirming symmetric from 2027-01-01 after Kep-00157/158 replaced Kep-00136. Whether Kep-00157/158 keep the phase-2 clause is unreported. Maybank page says symmetric from 2027-01-01 but also still says 15% to 2026-12-31 (stale).
- Effective date: sources say 2027-01-01 (calendar date); no source discusses first trading day. 2027-01-01 is a holiday so first trading day is 2027-01-04 (my inference; not from a source).

## Conclusion
Symmetric 2027 is the legislated/announced default plan (Kep-00136, pre-dating the 7% cut) but BEI itself called it subject to year-end market review, and Kep-00157/158 explicitly state validity to 2026-12-31 with evaluation. OPEN: revert to symmetric (35/25/20), extension of 7%, or something else. Needs official text check.
