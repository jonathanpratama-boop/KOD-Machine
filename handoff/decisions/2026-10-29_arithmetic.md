# 2026-10-29: the arithmetic of time to an answer, and what was done about it

**Owner instruction:** "I need you to solve the arithmetic problem. Make a solution if there isnt one."

**The problem.** A family needs `(t_min x sd / edge)^2` resolved pairs before its result means anything, and gets
them at its event rate. At the lab's rates the single families take years: SEC13D about 125 firing events a year, a
decade for a 2% edge; FORM4 about 1,500 a year, over a year for the 1% edge test 26 found.

**What was measured.** The spread of a set's paired excess, from test 26's already-reported scored events:
0.156 at hold 20 (prereg_2 had assumed 0.21), 0.098 for events above USD 20 at the same mean edge, 0.308 at hold 60.
Test 26's hit rate was 46.5% beside its positive mean: the insider-purchase edge is a minority of large winners.
Numbers and pins in `idx-signals/research/CATALYST_REGISTER_v1/POWER_v1/POWER.md`.

**Decisions.**
1. **DOCUMENT_CLASS, frozen** (`prereg_3_document_class.json`, `ADDENDUM_3.md`): a pooled, pre-registered test over
   the parent families SEC13D, DILUTION_8K and FORM4, each set's excess signed by its family's direction, mean
   primary, the same looks and t floor as prereg_2, futility from 240. About 2,400 events a year: a 1.5% class edge
   reads in about five months, 1% in about eleven. It answers "is there anything in the filings at all", not "which
   family to trade". Frozen before any resolution exists (the first H20 window ends 2026-11-27).
2. **The win rate is reported, not tested, for the class.** For a right-skewed signal a win-rate primary fails by
   construction. The frozen families keep their frozen primaries.
3. **The t >= 3 floor stays.** It costs about 40% more pairs than a conventional test; that is the price of
   thirty-five earlier tests and is stated, not paid down.
4. **Not done without the owner:** a price floor (signal close >= USD 20) as a family rule, the strongest variance
   lever; a discounted historical prior from test 26, which would cut the prospective sample by a quarter to a third
   but puts survivor-only data back into inference.

**What this means for "high win rate".** The one family with a lean is not a high-win-rate signal and cannot become
one by design; it is a positive-skew signal. A low losing rate, if it exists anywhere in this lab, will come from
abstention (the dilution veto, the sharpened filters), not from a buy rule.

**Not changed.** Every frozen protocol, prior, twin draw, resolution and ledger line; the kill test running in
parallel.
