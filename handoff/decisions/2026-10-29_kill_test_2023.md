## Outcome (2026-10-29, run at 06:48:57Z, after the pins were committed in 6b22481)

**NOT_KILLED, weakly.** The GO rule passed: 412 scored FIRE events against 300. The primary, mean excess over 20
sessions on FIRE events, is +1.01% (standard error 1.04%, t 0.97, one-sided p 0.17, 95% interval -0.96% to +3.10%),
median -0.12%, win rate 49.5% (Wilson 44.7% to 54.3%), mean net after costs +0.81%. The rule says KILL only at or
below zero, so the family stands. The evidence is consistent with a small positive edge, with zero, and with a small
negative one; survivor-only prices push the mean upward, so the true figure is more likely below +1% than above it.

**What that means for the prospective family.** At an edge of 1% or less, POWER_v1 puts a standalone SEC13D verdict
at t >= 3 about seventeen years away at its event rate. This test could not retire the family, but it gives no reason to
expect the family to reach significance on its own. Its sets still count toward DOCUMENT_CLASS, which is where this
family can contribute within a year.

**Descriptive, not counted.** The abstention rules separated the losers: ABSTAIN events averaged -0.07% (median
-3.5%, win rate 41.8%) against FIRE's +1.0% (median -0.1%, 49.5%); DILUTION_RECENT -0.56%, MV_UNKNOWN -0.96%,
MOVED_ALREADY -1.37% on six events. The avoid side of the register is doing visible work; the buy side is not
distinguishable from noise on this sample.

**Survivorship, stated.** 2,330 of 4,857 filings (48%) were dropped for having no ticker in the survivor-only panel.

**Recommendation.** Keep SEC13D and SEC13D_V2 sealing as contributors to the class, at their small EDGAR cost, and do
not expect a standalone verdict. The owner decides.
