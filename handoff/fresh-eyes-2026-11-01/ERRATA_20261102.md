# Errata to the FRESH_EYES documents (2026-11-02)

Corrections only. No result, protocol, ledger or pinned file was changed; the original wording stays in place and
is quoted below. Sources: the frozen historical replay journals (`historical_shared_account_results_20261101T1134`)
and the Sharpe-kill ablation (`handoff/ace-integration-20261101/sharpe_kill_ablation_20261101/REPORT.md`).

## 1. Sizing is per asset, not a portfolio-volatility target

`FRESH_EYES_VERDICT.md` §6 says "volatility-targeted sizing (daily portfolio volatility 1-2%)", and the "primary next
step" says "volatility-targeted to 1.5% daily". The implemented rule (`daily_core.py` lines 220-229) is:

- each long coin gets `min(1, 0.015 / sigma20)`, where `sigma20` is the standard deviation of its last 20 daily log
  returns;
- the pair is scaled down only if the weights sum above 1;
- there is no portfolio-volatility target and no correlation term anywhere in the code.

Recorded outcome: while holding coins, the account's daily volatility was **2.38% (development) and 2.55%
(validation)**, about 45-49% annualised, against the 1.5% per-coin target. In development the two coins were almost
uncorrelated (0.03) and each ran above its target; in validation they were highly correlated (0.84) and the
normalisation bound on 64 of 111 decisions.

## 2. "Preservation is solved by construction" and the loss cap were not built

§6 also lists "a hard daily and weekly loss cap enforced outside the strategy code". No such cap exists in the engine
or in the paper protocol. The 25% drawdown stop is not a cap either: it is checked only at daily opens and liquidates
at the following open. In the ablation it fired at 25.2% and 28.7% and the account ended at 27.1% and 30.2% below its
peak. (Ace's `PRIOR_ART_ADDENDUM.md`, in `ace-integration-20261101/paper_v2/.../fresh_eyes_audit_20261101/`, already
treats these as proposed mitigations, not a guarantee; this note records it where the original text lives.)

## 3. "Validated" means "survived a frozen historical test", not "shown to make money"

Lines 29, 161 and `HANDOFF_TO_ACE.md` line 57 call the rule "the best strategy validated here", "the validated rule"
and "the validated edge" (Sharpe about 1.1-1.2). Ace's independent audit
(`ace-integration-20261101/paper_v2/.../fresh_eyes_audit_20261101/INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md`, line 9) concludes: "ETH
SMA10/40 is a promising historical long/flat candidate, not demonstrated independent or prospective trading alpha.
BTC SMA10/40 is WEAK under the registered gate." Read the "validated" phrases as that: a historical research
candidate, not independently confirmed.

The audit (line 13) also finds that line 161's eToro claim, that "at eToro costs the validated rule no longer beats
buy-and-hold on Sharpe", reverses its own numbers: ETH exceeds both gates in both periods (Sharpe 1.132 vs 0.764
and 0.932 vs 0.653), while BTC's validation Sharpe is 0.854 vs 1.162. The claim that costs force a venue switch is
therefore not supported. That does not establish an edge either.

Note on contracts: the later shared-account replay is a different contract from the per-asset backtest above (it adds
risk-sized targets, shared cash and the kill rules). In it the trend account ended below conventional buy-and-hold on
ending value in both windows. That result neither confirms nor retests the claims in this section.

## 4. What "negative 90-day Sharpe" means

The paper protocol's kill criterion ("90-day Sharpe below zero") is implemented as: the mean of the last 90 daily
account returns is below zero, with no significance threshold (`daily_core.py` lines 241-247). With a nonzero
standard deviation, the sign of the Sharpe ratio is the sign of that mean. "Protocol declared FAILED" therefore means
"the loss rule tripped", not "the signal stopped working". In the frozen replay both trend accounts were stopped this
way while up 105.6% and 21.8% from their starts, on 90-day sums of -1.03% and -0.025% (t-statistics -0.06 and -0.001).
The frozen `PAPER_PROTOCOL.md` is not edited.

## 5. Two acceptance questions, kept apart

- Does the system execute and account for its rules correctly within the specified model? **Yes**, and independently
  audited.
- Is there adequate economic evidence that following those rules makes money after costs? **Not established.** About
  70 counted trials have produced no approved strategy.
