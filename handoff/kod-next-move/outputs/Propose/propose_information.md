# propose:information (Propose; agent a0e0ce4af7ef87a5a)

## title
E5_US_INFO_v1 (KOD-US-E5-v1): 8-K information timing - documented vs undocumented +30% days, and filing-type triggers as detectors
## angle
INFORMATION/CATALYST: condition on and detect primary-document timing (SEC EDGAR 8-K acceptance timestamps, item-typed) joined causally to the US daily panel; no tape threshold is changed, no new tape signal is searched.
## thesis
Every detector run so far (E1 F-family, E2 G-family, E3 plans, E4 shorts, US_BREAKOUT) read the tape only, and every PASS in the paper ledger literally says 'no news or filing was read' (idx-signals/idxsig/paper.py:574). The KOD doctrine says repricings follow information and that a primary document before price is the detector-grade fact; the only test of that idea is one retrospective event (ZEALAND) and four retrospective cards. Hypothesis H1 (Track A, the +30% day with vs without a document): among G2_RET30 flags (day return >= +30%, code copied verbatim), the flags whose session carries an 8-K accepted by EDGAR between the prior close and 15:45 ET of the flag session ('documented spikes', I1_DOC_RET30) reprice again (+30% max close within t+1..t+5) more often and lose less under the E2 plan than flags with no 8-K in the last six sessions ('undocumented spikes', I2_NODOC_RET30). Mechanism: a documented move is a repricing of a public fact that other holders have not finished digesting (post-event drift), whereas an undocumented +30% day in a survivor-only US micro-cap universe is more often a liquidity/pump episode that mean-reverts; the matched controls cannot see this because docs are not a control feature. Hypothesis H2 (Track B, filing-type trigger alone): an item-typed 8-K (1.01 material agreement, 2.01 completed transaction, 7.01 Reg FD, 8.01 other events) accepted while the price is still quiet (|ret1| <= 5%, volume <= 2x median) - the population form of KOD-DET-v0 PRIMARY_DOC_BEFORE_PRICE - has E30 precision at least 2x matched random controls (same session, price band, liquidity quintile, volatility tercile). Both are tested with discovery 2020-07..2022, one validation pass on 2023, and a prospective sidecar log that records the same doc fields for every live US paper decision from the day the protocol is frozen.
## why_not_already_falsified
Different information source: E1/E2/E3/E4 and the breakout baseline used only the 12 exchange fields a rule may see (idx-signals/idxsig/rules/__init__.py:89-90); no experiment has ever joined a filing, a press release or an earnings date to the panel, and controls.py v2 cannot match on documents, so the document dimension is orthogonal to every lift and every loss recorded so far. Different structure: Track A is a pre-registered two-arm contrast of an exogenous binary (EDGAR clock), not a new threshold or plan - E3 searched 108 plans and E4 288 short plans on the same tape; E3_US_RETENTION (unrun) conditions on close(t+1), still tape. Track B has no tape trigger at all; the only earlier non-tape detector evaluation is KOD-DET-v0 on 4 retrospective cards (all NO_SIGNAL) and the single ZEALAND event, which the pilot itself says cannot estimate a rate and which is excluded here by construction (US only, 2020-2023). The map's one pre-event separator (20-session volatility, AUC 0.78 US) is matched away in this design's detection controls (E5-MATCH-v1 adds the volatility tercile to E1-MATCH-v1), so a filing lift cannot be 'volatile names file more'. The expected sign is not foregone: E2's pooled result (median 5-session return -10.3% after a G2 flag, US 2023) is consistent with both 'documented spikes drift' and 'documented spikes are complete at the open'; either outcome changes what the paper policy may say in a PASS reason, and a null kills the doctrine's document criterion as a daily-bar US detector input.
## protocol
### family
I-family, idxsig/rules/moonshot_i.py (new, registered in rules.registry()), 8 candidates fixed before any number. Tape features are a verbatim copy of moonshot_g.features (tests assert identical flags to G2_RET30 etc. on a synthetic panel, as tests/test_e3.py does for E1); information fields come from new rule-visible Market fields built from EDGAR 8-K acceptance times. SELECTED = None until the freeze; harness rule ids E5_CAND_<name>; after the freeze MOONSHOT_E5_US_D1 (Track B selected) and SELECTED_CONTRAST = ('I1_DOC_RET30','I2_NODOC_RET30') frozen as records.
### detectors
- I1_DOC_RET30: ret1 >= 0.30 (G2_RET30 verbatim) AND doc_any(t)=1 (an 8-K accepted in (16:00 ET t-1, 15:45 ET t])
- I2_NODOC_RET30: ret1 >= 0.30 AND doc_any(t)=0 AND doc_last(t) > 5 (no 8-K in sessions t-5..t); names without a CIK map are NaN (not evaluable) in both arms
- I3_PREDOC_RET30 (descriptive only, not tested): ret1 >= 0.30 AND doc_pre(t)=1 (8-K accepted in (16:00 ET t-1, 09:30 ET t]: the move began as a gap on the document)
- I4_DOC_P: doc_cls_p(t)=1 (8-K with any of items 1.01, 2.01, 7.01, 8.01 accepted in the window), tradeable, no tape condition
- I5_DOC_P_QUIET: doc_cls_p(t)=1 AND |ret1| <= 0.05 AND volr <= 2 (KOD PRIMARY_DOC_BEFORE_PRICE in population form: document public, price not repriced, volume not surged)
- I6_DOC_E: doc_cls_e(t)=1 (item 2.02 earnings release 8-K in the window), no tape condition
- I7_DOC_P_VOLCREEP: doc_cls_p(t)=1 AND |ret1| <= 0.05 AND median volume t-4..t / median volume t-24..t-5 >= 1.5 (ZEALAND protocol's frozen volume-creep fact, 2026-10-08, applied on the document session)
- I8_DOC_N (negative control, pre-registered expectation lift <= 1): doc_cls_n(t)=1 (items 3.01 delisting notice, 3.02 unregistered sales, 1.03 bankruptcy, 2.04 triggering event, 4.02 non-reliance, 5.03 charter amendment), no tape condition
### plans
exactly one plan, E2's, for every rule and every control: buy at the open of s+1 (US profile entry_price='open', entry_lag 1), sell at the close of the 5th session after entry (holding_days 5), no stop, overlap skip, $5,000 notional, lot 1. No plan grid is searched (E3/E4 showed plan search on these tapes is exhausted; this experiment isolates the information variable).

### discovery_period
2020-07-01 .. 2022-12-31 (E2's discovery window; in-sample for G2_RET30's threshold, which is not re-tuned; the document split is new information never joined to any outcome)
### validation_period
2023-01-01 .. 2023-12-29, one pass, after the freeze commit. Track B (information-only detector) treats 2023 as a clean validation year: no filing-based rule was ever selected on it. Track A is labelled explicitly 'a stratified re-read of a year whose pooled G2_RET30 outcomes were seen (E2, 9 reruns)': the direction is fixed by doctrine before any number so it cannot be chosen on 2023, but its evidential weight is lower than Track B's and the decisive Track A evidence is the prospective sidecar (see admissibility).
### admissibility
Track A (contrast, discovery): both arms >= 200 flags, else NOT EVALUABLE (reported, neither supported nor falsified). Supported on discovery iff (a) P(E30|I1) - P(E30|I2) >= +3.0 pp with one-sided two-proportion z p < 0.05 in the pre-registered direction I1 > I2, and (b) avg net(I1) - avg net(I2) under the one plan > 0 with session-block bootstrap p < 0.05 (2,000 resamples, seed 20261007). I1 is additionally an executable candidate only if it meets E3's rule: scored >= 200, avg net > 0, one-sided p < 0.05 vs matched random, avg net > 0 in every discovery year with >= 50 scored. Track B (information-only, discovery): admissible iff flags >= 200, Wilson 95% lower bound of E30 precision > base rate, lift vs E5-MATCH-v1 matched random >= 2.0 with one-sided p < 0.05 (200 draws per flag, floor 0.005); the admissible candidate with the highest lift is selected (ties by flags); trading run only for the selected one, judged by E3's rule above; I8 must show lift <= 1 or the item-class mapping is flagged as suspect (reported, not judged). Validation 2023 (one pass): Track A passes iff the same (a) and (b) hold; Track B selected passes iff detection Wilson lower bound > base rate, lift >= 2.0, p < 0.05 AND trading scored >= 100, avg net > 0, p < 0.05 vs matched. Prospective arm: from the freeze date the US paper routine appends, for every US DECISION, the same doc fields retrieved from data.sec.gov at decision time (retrieval time recorded, hash-chained, never read by decide()); first prospective contrast reported at 200 scored US decisions, judged by (a) only. Consequence if passed: a new paper policy version may cite the document fact in US decisions; no BUY until the executable plan also passes. Consequence if failed: EDGAR 8-K timing is dropped from the detector roadmap; holdout stays sealed.
### costs_model
profiles.US unchanged: per side 1.5% under $1, 0.5% $1-5, 0.15% $5-20, 0.05% above, SEC fee on sales, no impact, no dividends; fill model B (FILL-B-v1: 10% of the 20-session median as-traded volume through the signal, decision-time), units as_traded from the repair panel's unit_factor, quarantine none (the repair_v3 finding that quarantine is outcome-correlated), with quarantine unit_schedule reported as a sensitivity, not judged. Unverified pre-2023 split units affect cost tier and lot size only (max 1.45% per side); both arms and all controls share it.
### matched_controls
Trading stage: controls v2 exactly as the harness computes them (same session; price band, liquidity quintile, market cap quintile [one bucket for US], volatility tercile, momentum tercile, drawdown tercile, listing age; relaxed to >= 5 names), 200 draws per attempted signal, executed under the identical plan, plus the same-session uniform baseline. Detection stage: E5-MATCH-v1 = E1-MATCH-v1 (same session, price band, liquidity quintile) plus 20-session volatility tercile, relaxed in that order, 200 draws per flag; the doc share among drawn controls is reported so contamination is visible. For Track A the primary comparison is arm vs arm; each arm's matched baseline is reported beside it.

## falsifier
On discovery 2020-07-01..2022-12-31, with both arms >= 200 flags: P(E30 | I1_DOC_RET30) - P(E30 | I2_NODOC_RET30) < +3.0 pp or its one-sided two-proportion z-test (direction I1 > I2) gives p >= 0.05; AND no information-only candidate among I4..I8 with >= 200 flags reaches E30 lift >= 2.0 vs E5-MATCH-v1 matched random at one-sided p < 0.05. That exact pair of results means 8-K timing carries no detectable information about a +30% repricing at the daily-bar level in this universe; no validation run is made, the family is closed, and EDGAR timing leaves the detector roadmap. (If either Track A arm has < 200 flags the contrast is recorded NOT EVALUABLE, which neither supports nor falsifies H1.) If discovery passes, the 2023 pass fails and the hypothesis is rejected when the contrast's sign reverses or p >= 0.05, or when the selected Track B detector's Wilson lower bound on 2023 does not exceed the base rate.
## risks
- Look-ahead in filing timestamps: (1) EDGAR file_date rolls to the next business day for acceptances after 17:30 ET, so file_date is never used for attribution, only acceptanceDateTime; (2) the submissions JSON acceptanceDateTime carries a 'Z' suffix whose meaning (UTC vs the Eastern time shown on filing-index pages) must be pinned on a 50-filing sample before the freeze, else every attribution can be off by one session; (3) acceptance precedes public dissemination by seconds to minutes during 06:00-22:00 ET, hence the 15-minute pre-close cutoff (15:45 ET) and the 15-minute buffer are pre-registered; (4) the 8-K is an upper bound on the public time: the wire press release can precede the furnished 8-K by hours or days (issuers have four business days), and some releases are never furnished, so 'no document' means 'no EDGAR document', a misclassification that biases Track A toward the null (a positive finding is conservative, a null is weaker evidence).
- Survivor-only universe (Nasdaq Trader 2026 snapshot): names delisted 2020-2026 are absent, and pump-type undocumented spikes are plausibly over-represented among delistings, so the I2 arm is the surviving subset; the sign of the bias on the contrast is unknown and is stated, not corrected.
- CIK discontinuities (de-SPACs, re-domiciliations, holding-company reorganisations): 8-Ks filed under a predecessor CIK are missed, misclassifying those sessions as undocumented; the info-panel manifest reports mapped/unmapped counts and formerNames, but the residual is unknown.
- Reachability and rate limits of data.sec.gov from the sandbox are untested (only companyconcept was reached before); ~6,000 requests at 5/s; fallback is the bulk submissions.zip; a placeholder User-Agent (HANDOFF item 8) must be replaced before any pull.
- Harness change surface: extending RULE_FIELDS and Market.FIELDS touches the causal contract; a defect there could leak information to every rule, so the lookahead test (doc at d+1 must raise LookaheadError) and the reproduce stage (recorded E2 run byte-identical on the info panel) are preconditions, not afterthoughts.
- Multiplicity: 8 candidates and one contrast; controlled by freezing the family, the lift >= 2.0 floor, the +3 pp materiality floor and a single validation pass; broad information-only detectors (I4/I6 may have tens of thousands of flags) are kept out of the trading stage by the lift floor, which also bounds compute (the detection evaluate loop at e1.py:334-376 is per flag).
- Track A's 2023 validation is a consumed year for G2_RET30's pooled trading outcome; the pre-registered direction cannot be gamed but the weight of a 2023 pass is lower than a clean year; the prospective sidecar is slow (about 200 US decisions a month at the observed scan rate, doc share unknown), so a first prospective contrast with ~+/-5 pp half-width is months away.
- Item-class map is a judgement fixed before data (1.01/2.01/7.01/8.01 as 'material', 2.02 earnings, 3.01/3.02/1.03/2.04/4.02/5.03 as 'distress/dilution'); if I8 shows lift > 1 the map is flagged suspect (reported), not re-cut.
- Execution realism unchanged from E2: daily bars, assumed tier costs, no auction size, no LULD halts; a positive Track A trading contrast is a difference between two arms under the same assumptions, not a tradeable edge until the executable plan passes E3's rule and APPROVAL-v2.
- Crypto and IDX cannot be covered now (no reachable announcement or disclosure history), so this is one market, one document type; a null does not falsify the doctrine for IDX disclosures or crypto listings.

## expected_information_value
Whatever the sign, the experiment answers three questions the lab has never asked with a population and non-events: (1) does a primary document on the spike session change the post-spike path (if I1 > I2: information-conditioned detection is the next detector axis and the paper PASS reason gains an evidence-based catalyst field; if I1 = I2: 8-K timing is not operational at daily-bar resolution for US micro-caps and the document criterion leaves the roadmap; if I1 < I2 significantly: documented spikes are complete by the next open and undocumented ones carry the continuation, which points the E4 short family at I1 and a long family at I2, both new protocols); (2) the leak diagnostic (share of undocumented spikes followed by an 8-K within five sessions and their E30 rate) measures whether pre-announcement informed trading is a visible mechanism in this universe, a fact KOD's VOLUME_CREEP and PRIMARY_DOC detectors depend on; (3) Track B tells whether any filing-type trigger has detection lift net of volatility matching, i.e. whether PRIMARY_DOC_BEFORE_PRICE can ever be a scanner input rather than an operator fact, and I8 calibrates the item-class map. It also delivers reusable infrastructure: a point-in-time 8-K timing column with provenance, a tested RULE_FIELDS extension path for any future non-price input, and a prospective catalyst log attached to every live US decision, which is the watchlist source the KOD reader says the lab lacks.
