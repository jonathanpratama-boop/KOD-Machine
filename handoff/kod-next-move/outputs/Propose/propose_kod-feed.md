# propose:kod-feed (Propose; agent ab217d016c0c098b1)

## title
KPF-v1: daily tape-fed prospective KOD pipeline (whole-universe scan + STRICT signal/control cases) in IDX, US and crypto
## angle
KOD PROSPECTIVE PIPELINE: open and close AUTHORITATIVE prospective KOD cases every day from the bars the paper-scan routines already pull, with T-24H discipline, KOD-EVIDENCE-v2 objects, RFC 3161 anchoring, GitHub receipts and mechanical outcome scoring; no trades, no backtest.
## thesis
Two claims, one infrastructure. Scientific claim (the only KOD-DET-v0 detector whose inputs are tape-derivable): VOLUME_CREEP_PRICE_QUIET, evaluated at T-24H with the ZEALAND_PILOT_v1 fact definitions frozen 2026-10-08 (median volume of the last 5 sessions / median of the 20 before >= 1.5; median range ratio over 5 sessions <= 1.0 and |ret5| <= 2 x vol20 x sqrt(5)), precedes a >= +30% close-to-close repricing within the next 5 sessions more often than the base rate of the same universe. The mechanism that would make it true is informed accumulation ahead of an undisclosed catalyst (the H-B observation in kod-machine/research/ZEALAND_PILOT_v1/05_findings/FINDINGS.md, recorded as untested). Infrastructure claim: the full STRICT-v3 chain (evidence stamped before the cutoff, card opened within the hour, anchor pushed, GitHub push-run receipt dated before the window) can run unattended once per market per session, so that the doctrine's floor of 30 closed prospective cases is reached in about two weeks of operation instead of never, and the KOD scan log supplies the prospective base rate and the non-events that doctrine lines 79-81 require. Each case asks one pre-registered question: given the T-24H tape facts for SYMBOL (through session d-1) and no document facts (UNKNOWN), will the close reprice by >= +30% (REPRICED_UP) or <= -30% (REPRICED_DOWN) relative to the close of session d within sessions d+1..d+5?
## why_not_already_falsified
Signal family: E1/E2 measured a single-day volume surge (volr = volume / prior-20-session median, matched-set AUC 0.50-0.52) and 20-session volatility (0.68/0.78/0.56); neither measured the KOD conjunction (5-vs-20 median volume creep AND range quiet relative to the name's own median AND |ret5| inside 2 sigma), and E1/E2's prior is if anything against it (quiet names reprice less), so the result is informative either way. Information source and structure: every existing experiment is a backtest on consumed discovery/validation windows; this is prospective, on 2026 live sessions that are not holdout, with a whole-universe scan base rate (never measured prospectively for any market) and matched control cases, which no backtest in the repo can produce for a KOD detector because KOD facts are not RuleView fields. The paper ledger tests the opposite population (post-spike G2_RET30/F1_ARA continuation, 1 scored outcome so far); E3/E4 test execution plans on those same post-spike detectors; the breakout baseline is a 60-session closing-high rule. KOD's own record is 0 prospective cases, 0 scans, 0 GitHub receipts (kod-machine/var/kod.db: cases 4, scan_runs 0, remote_timestamp_receipts 0), so nothing about KOD-DET-v0 has been tested or falsified, and the chain itself has only run with tests/support.py FakeTimestamper and FakeGitHub.
## protocol
### family
KPF-v1 (KOD prospective feed). Scanner id KPF-TAPE-v1 per market, logged with `kod scan`; cases KOD-CARD-v3 STRICT-v3 opened with `kod open`; detectors KOD-DET-v0 unchanged (kod-machine/kod/detectors.py, sha256 0a0e0a73...; require_detector_unchanged refuses otherwise); fact definitions copied verbatim from kod-machine/research/ZEALAND_PILOT_v1/01_protocol/protocol.json fact_definitions and 03_card/price_facts.py measures(); protocol frozen as kod-machine/research/KPF_v1/{PROTOCOL.md,protocol.json,FREEZE.json} with RFC 3161 tokens (the ZEALAND 01_protocol pattern) before the first evidence object, protocol sha written into every scan's notes and every card's story_hypothesis (hashed).
### detectors
- VOLUME_CREEP_PRICE_QUIET: the tested detector; inputs volume_creep_before_story and price_quiet_vs_own_range computed at the last session at or before the T-24H cutoff (session d-1) from the stamped snapshot chain
- COVERAGE_INCOMPLETE: evaluated; SIGNAL by construction (reconstruction_status PARTIAL_PUBLIC: vendor daily bars only, no filings/news/order book)
- DATED_CATALYST_KNOWN, PRIMARY_DOC_BEFORE_PRICE, NARRATIVE_WITHOUT_MECHANISM: INSUFFICIENT_DATA by construction in v1 (document facts dated_catalyst_known, primary_document_public, narrative_only, mechanism_in_primary_docs are null = UNKNOWN; price_already_repriced is computed and cited but its partner fact is null). Populating them needs a v1.1 document feed (EDGAR acceptance times from Archives headers for the US cohort) under a new scanner id, not a KOD-DET edit
- FLAG:KPF-TAPE-v1 (the scanner flag row): VOLUME_CREEP_PRICE_QUIET SIGNAL at T-24H AND price_already_repriced false; hit_label REPRICED_UP
### plans
No trade plan: KOD claims no fill, no MFE/MAE as profit (doctrine lines 74-77). Outcome plan, pre-registered in protocol.json and in each card: reference price = close(d), the last close before the decision cutoff; REPRICED_UP iff max close over sessions d+1..d+5 >= 1.30 x close(d); REPRICED_DOWN iff min close over d+1..d+5 <= 0.70 x close(d); if both, the one crossed first by session order; otherwise NO_REPRICE; CENSORED if fewer than 5 post-d sessions of bars exist 10 sessions after d (delisting, halt, untraded); UNKNOWN if units cannot be established (US: any provider split record effective after d that is not SEC-VERIFIED under us-actions-v2, exactly paper-score-v3's UNSCORABLE rule; any market: a close/previous ratio > 10 or < 0.1 inside the window; the snapshot close(d) not reconciling with the scoring bars within rtol 1e-3). window_return_pct = close(d+5)/close(d) - 1 (descriptive only). Timing per market (all UTC; decision_cutoff must be within 1 hour of the open and the window must start after both, ledger.py:195-217): US paper scan 22:53Z -> snapshot evidence as_of 23:30:00Z d -> scan and cards decision_cutoff 23:30:00Z d, T-24H 23:30Z d-1, T-72H 23:30Z d-3, T-30D 23:30Z d-30, window start 00:00:00Z d+1, end 00:00Z of the calendar day after the fifth weekday after d; IDX paper scan 10:26Z -> as_of/cutoff 11:00:00Z d, window start 12:00Z d; CRYPTO paper scan 00:25Z d+1 (UTC day d) -> as_of/cutoff 01:00:00Z d+1, T-24H 01:00Z d (last complete kline = day d-1), window start 02:00Z d+1, end 01:00Z d+6. A late routine (stamp after the slot) logs the scan but opens no cases that day (pre-registered skip, never a shifted cutoff). Case selection, deterministic and outcome-blind: per market per session K=2 signal cases = flagged entries ranked by sha256(scan_id + '|' + symbol), plus K=2 matched controls (below); disposition WATCH for all (templated critic note: loss path = base-rate NO_REPRICE or a down repricing; missing evidence = every document fact; invalidation = the pre-registered outcome rule); story_hypothesis carries the question text, the numeric features at T-24H, the control/signal role and the protocol sha.
### markets
- CRYPTO: universe = every name the paper scan marks eligible on the Binance USDT spot panel (about 480/day); 2022 discovery-session sizing count: 7.4 flags/day of 314 evaluable (2.4%), VOLUME_CREEP SIGNAL 2.7%
- IDX: universe = every eligible name of the IDX paper scan (about 830/day); sizing count on 2022: 31 flags/day of 602 evaluable (5.2%), SIGNAL 5.5%; names without high/low are INSUFFICIENT_DATA and never selected
- US: universe = the pre-registered 20% ticker-hash cohort of the eligible set (ticker in cohort iff int(sha256('KPF-v1|US|'+ticker)[:8],16) % 5 == 0; about 1,100/day), chosen only to bound storage (each scan entry costs about 1.5 KB of pretty-printed registry JSON plus 6 kod.db rows, and var/kod.db is committed); sizing count on 2022 full universe: 143 flags/day of 4,484 (3.2%), so about 29/day in the cohort; the full universe is KPF-US-v2 once 60 sessions of storage cost are measured
### discovery_period
None. No historical outcome is read or computed. The only historical numbers are flag COUNTS per day on 2022 discovery sessions (US repair panel, crypto panel, IDX panel rows dated 2021-10-01..2022-12-31 for warm-up), taken to size the pipeline; they are not an outcome statistic and selected nothing.
### validation_period
Prospective only: from launch (target 2026-10-27 after a SIMULATED dress rehearsal) for 60 sessions per market (pre-registered readout at the first `kod report` after 60 AUTHORITATIVE closed scans: about 2026-12-26 crypto, 2027-01-26 US/IDX). The sealed holdouts (IDX 2024-01-02..2025-02-21; US/crypto 2024 onward) are never loaded; 2026 live bars are not holdout under research/E2/protocol.json's wording but the proposal reads nothing before launch day except the 60-session history object H0 built from the routines' own recent pulls (data/raw/*_recent, 200 days), which does not touch 2024-2025 rows because the pulls start 2026-03-28 or later.
### admissibility
Acceptance criteria (infrastructure, evaluated at launch + 20 trading days): A1 >= 30 AUTHORITATIVE closed prospective cases with a scored label (not UNKNOWN/CENSORED) in kod report (score.py PROMOTION_MIN = 30; AUTHORITATIVE per verify.py:96-133 = STRICT card, anchor commit ancestor of HEAD, GitHub push run created before outcome_window.start); A2 every KPF scan AUTHORITATIVE and closed within 3 sessions of its window end (scan_overdue_unclosed = 0); A3 `kod verify` ok and CI green (tools/check_append_only.py --all) throughout; A4 share of UNKNOWN outcomes <= 20% per market. Pre-registered scientific readout R1 per market at 60 closed scans, on the scan lift table for VOLUME_CREEP_PRICE_QUIET with REPRICED_UP: s = signal hit rate (n_s expected about 440 crypto, 1,700 US-cohort, 1,860 IDX), b = no-signal hit rate; SUPPORTED iff the Wilson 95% lower bound of s > b (lift > 1 established); FALSIFIED iff the Wilson 95% upper bound of s < 2b (minimal interesting lift 2 excluded); otherwise INCONCLUSIVE and the feed continues another 60 sessions without any change. The case detector_table (signal vs matched-control cases) is reported alongside and is never the decision statistic at n = 30. Promotion is out of scope: doctrine line 65 requires complete coverage and v1 cases are PARTIAL_PUBLIC; a SUPPORTED readout can only motivate KOD-DET-v1 with a new cohort.
### matched_controls
Two layers. (1) Scan base rate: every universe name is a scan entry with the same T-24H facts and the same outcome rule, so each detector and the FLAG row get both sides and a lift (score.py lift_table). (2) Control cases: for each signal case, one NO_SIGNAL case (VOLUME_CREEP_PRICE_QUIET NO_SIGNAL with both facts non-null, price_already_repriced false) drawn by the same hash rank from the same session's universe in the same price band (profile band edges: IDX 200/5,000; US 1/5/20; CRYPTO 0.01/1/100, from idxsig/profiles.py) and the same liquidity quintile (adv20_value from the paper features file, quintiles within the day's universe); relaxed to band only, then to any, with the relaxation written into the story. Controls are opened as STRICT cards under the identical outcome plan.
### costs_model
None. KOD records no entry, fill, cost or profit; window_return_pct is a descriptive close-to-close number. Execution feasibility of anything found is a separate idxsig protocol (EXEC-US-v2.1 / E3-style plan grid) with its own matched random baseline.

## falsifier
Infrastructure falsifier: 20 trading days after launch, kod report shows fewer than 30 AUTHORITATIVE closed prospective cases with a scored label, or any KPF scan that is not AUTHORITATIVE, or kod verify not ok. That kills the claim that the STRICT chain can be run unattended at this cadence, and the causes (TSA refusals, pushes landing after window start, UNKNOWN units) are visible in the excluded_prospective table. Scientific falsifier (pre-registered R1): at 60 AUTHORITATIVE closed scans in a market, the Wilson 95% upper bound of VOLUME_CREEP_PRICE_QUIET's signal hit rate for REPRICED_UP is below 2 x the no-signal hit rate of the same market; if that holds in all three markets, the tape half of the KOD hypothesis is dead at KOD-DET-v0 thresholds and no threshold is touched on these outcomes (a new threshold set is KOD-DET-v1 with a new cohort). Expected n at 60 sessions: about 440 signal entries crypto (base about 2.4%), 1,700 US-cohort (about 1.3%), 1,860 IDX (about 1.2%), which excludes lift 2 when the observed signal rate equals the base rate.
## risks
- Storage: each scan entry is about 1.5 KB of registry JSON plus 6 rows in the committed var/kod.db; at 480+830+1,100 entries/day the database grows roughly 3-4 MB/day (about 200 MB over the 60-session pilot, more if git deltas are poor); the US cohort cap and gzip scan bodies bound it, but the full US universe (5,600/day) is not affordable in this ledger design
- Timestamp authorities: both freetsa and sectigo down at the slot refuses every open that day (anchor.py:135-137); 40 stamps/day may hit unknown rate limits; a missed slot means no cases (never a shifted cutoff)
- Receipts: the anchor push must reach GitHub before outcome_window.start (30-60 minutes after the cutoff); a failed or delayed push makes the case REMOTE_TIMESTAMP_LATE/UNVERIFIED and it is excluded; GitHub Actions run records expire after about 410 days, so reports must be taken before then
- Doctrine line 65 'complete coverage': every v1 card is PARTIAL_PUBLIC, COVERAGE_INCOMPLETE fires on all, three of five detectors are INSUFFICIENT_DATA on all; v1 can falsify or support VOLUME_CREEP_PRICE_QUIET but cannot promote anything; whether the owner accepts tape-only cases as satisfying the floor needs a doctrine decision (new version, new sha)
- The 24-hour staleness built into KOD loses one session between the last fact (d-1) and the first outcome session (d+1); E2 found a median lead of 1 session to peak in the US, so one-session-lead events are structurally invisible to this design
- Units and data: US survivor-only universe (new listings enter the cohort by hash; delisted names vanish, CENSORED); Yahoo bars revised after the snapshot make reconcile failures (UNKNOWN); IDX names without high/low are never evaluable; holidays are unmodelled so closes wait for bars
- Multiplicity: three markets and two lift rows (FLAG and detector) are read at R1; the readout rule is per market and pre-registered, but a single-market SUPPORTED result is weak evidence and must not be sold as validation
- Operational: the routine host needs a GitHub token with actions:read and SEC egress; the repo has no scheduler, so the wiring lives outside the repository and its failure modes are invisible to CI
- The scan FLAG row and the VOLUME_CREEP detector row overlap by construction (FLAG adds only price_already_repriced false); readers must not count them as two findings
## directive_compliance
Holdout: never loaded; the feed runs on 2026 live sessions from launch day forward, the history object H0 comes from the routines' 2026 recent pulls, and the only historical computation in this proposal was a flag count on 2022 discovery rows with no outcome read (IDX 2024-01-02..2025-02-21 and US/crypto 2024-2025 rows untouched). No re-tune: KOD-DET-v0 code (sha 0a0e0a73..., bound in detector_versions and enforced by require_detector_unchanged) and the ZEALAND fact definitions frozen 2026-10-08 are reused verbatim; the cohort hash, K, matching rule, outcome thresholds and the readout rule are fixed in protocol.json before the first evidence object; a SUPPORTED or FALSIFIED readout changes nothing in detectors.py, and any new threshold is KOD-DET-v1 on a new cohort. No manufactured results: this proposal reports counts, not probabilities; the only expected numbers are sample sizes and base rates quoted from E1/E2 validation as historical reference, and the prior from E1/E2 (volume surge AUC 0.50) is stated as unfavourable. Pre-registration: protocol frozen and RFC 3161-stamped (ZEALAND FREEZE.json pattern) before any scan or card; every card is hashed, anchored, stamped and pushed before its window; outcomes are appended only after window end with the card_sha from the open; registry and research directories are append-only under tools/check_append_only.py; no real orders, no ASTRA or paper-policy change, no edit to idxsig/paper.py.
## expected_information_value
Either way the lab gets its first AUTHORITATIVE prospective records: a proven end-to-end STRICT chain (or a documented list of where it breaks), prospective per-market base rates for +30%/5-session repricings on the full eligible universe (never measured outside backtests), and the first both-sides hit-rate table for a KOD-DET-v0 detector. If R1 is FALSIFIED in all three markets, the quiet-volume-creep hypothesis is closed at these thresholds and KOD's remaining detectors are known to require a document feed (EDGAR acceptance times) rather than more tape work. If R1 is SUPPORTED in any market, it is the first pre-event separator found prospectively in the project (E1/E2 found only prior volatility, on seen data), which would justify a KOD-DET-v1 cohort and, separately, an idxsig execution-plan protocol with matched random baselines on the same flags. The infrastructure outlives the hypothesis: the same snapshot-evidence, scan, card and close steps carry any future scanner id (document-fed or tape-fed) at a marginal cost of one routine step per market. Timeline estimate: build 48 hours over about 8 working days from 2026-10-15, protocol freeze 2026-10-24, SIMULATED rehearsal 2026-10-26, first real opens 2026-10-27, 12 cases/day across markets so 30 cases are open by the third session; windows end 5 sessions after each signal day and closes run on the next routine, so 30 AUTHORITATIVE closed cases are expected by about 2026-11-06 (crypto) and 2026-11-09 (IDX/US), conditional on TSA, push and receipts succeeding; the 60-session readout R1 falls around 2026-12-26 (crypto) and 2027-01-26 (IDX/US).
