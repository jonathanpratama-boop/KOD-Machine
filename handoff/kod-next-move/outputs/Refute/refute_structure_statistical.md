# refute:structure:statistical (Refute; agent a2ad45838ed1f1c34)

## refuted
True
## severity
serious
## reasons
- REDENOMINATION ARTIFACTS DECIDE THE CRYPTO GRID AS WRITTEN. The protocol asserts 'redenomination-style prints are handled by the panel's spike drops only' and declines a jump exclusion ('jump_ratio not applicable (long, 1x)'). Checked on the proposal's own panel (data/panel/panel_crypto-20261008T003354Z.csv.gz, discovery rows only): global_panel._spikes drops only one-day spikes that revert the next day; a permanent level change passes, and because `previous` is chained to the pre-redenomination close, Market.build's reset test (last_close/ref) sees ratio 1, so under ca_mode='adjusted' the harness scores the jump as a return. Four G2_RET30-flagged names inside discovery carry such jumps: COCOS (flag 2021-01-15; halted 01-20..01-22; 2021-01-23 close 0.9003 vs previous 0.000695, a 1000:1 redenomination E4 v1 already documented), DREP (flags 2021-03-05 and 2021-03-19; 2021-04-02 close/previous = 107.6x), SUN (flag 2021-06-03; 2021-06-18 close/previous = 0.0012) and VIDT (flag 2022-10-19; 2022-11-09 ratio 0.051). Gross per-trade ratios these single flags would contribute to the 15 crypto cells: COCOS +126,000% (lag1/h5), +94,000%/+80,000% (lag1 h10/h20), +97,000..+164,000% in every lag-2 and lag-3 cell, SKIP_NOT_TRADED at lag 6, real at lag 11; DREP 03-05: +22,881% (lag6/h20), +13,771% (lag11/h20); DREP 03-19: +9,200..+10,400% in lag1/h10, lag1/h20, lag2/h10, lag2/h20, lag3/h10, lag3/h20, and +9,558..+13,288% in lag6/h5, lag6/h10, lag6/h20. So 13 of the 15 crypto cells (all but lag11/h5 and lag11/h10) contain at least one non-trade of +9,000% to +164,000%; with ~1,200 scored trades per cell (~750 in 2021; E4 reference row: 1,244 scored, 748 in 2021) a +10,000% trade adds about +8pp to the cell mean and +13pp to the 2021 mean, COCOS about +100pp/+170pp. The admissibility criteria 'avg net > 0', 'avg net > 0 in 2021' and the selection rule 'highest average net return' are then determined by which cells happen to span a redenomination, not by the hypothesis; the matched draws are contaminated at random (E4's reference row shows matched p2.5 = -108% for the mirrored short), so the p<0.05 test is noisy too. E4 v2 fixed exactly this with jump_ratio=10 (EXCLUDED_DATA_JUMP, applied to rule and baselines alike, origin/claude/e4-fade-short-side execution.py L87/L532-540), which would catch all five jumps (ratios 1295, 107.6, 0.0012, 0.051). A protocol change rescues it, so serious rather than fatal; as proposed, the crypto discovery table would be uninterpretable.
- THE DECISION STATISTIC IS A RAW PER-TRADE MEAN WITH 'HIGHEST MEAN' SELECTION ACROSS HOLDS UP TO 20 SESSIONS IN CRYPTO 2021. Even after excluding redenominations, E4's record shows real 5-session squeezes of +200% to +424% (GMT, WRX, DOGE, NKN, CHR, STMX) on these flags; at 20 sessions the right tail is fatter still. The selection rule will pick the cell containing the largest single realized winner, i.e. the noisiest cell, and 'avg net > 0 in 2021' is met by the tail rather than by drift (E4 v1: 0 of 144 short plans positive in 2021, i.e. every long-equivalent plan was positive in the bull year). E3's rule is kept 'for comparability', but E3 searched IDX holds of 2-5 sessions with price limits; it was not designed for unbounded 20-session crypto tails. No robustness co-criterion (winsorized/trimmed mean, or positivity after removing the top 1% of trades) is pre-registered.
- THE FALSIFIER'S INFERENCE IS LOGICALLY INVALID. The hypothesis is relative ('flagged names drift up relative to matched names') but admissibility requires an ABSOLUTE positive average in every discovery year with >=50 scored, and 2020 (110 scored at lag1/h5), 2021 and 2022 all bind on crypto. 2022 is a -65% BTC year; E4's record shows 143 of 144 mirrored plans positive for shorts in 2022 at holds <=5, and a 10-20 session matched long in 2022 will itself be negative. A cell that beats matched by a wide margin in 2022 can therefore fail admissibility, after which the protocol declares 'the flagged names keep losing versus matched names at every entry timing' and closes the family. 'No admissible cell' does not imply 'losing vs matched'; the mechanism verdict rests on the wrong statistic, while the only relative-by-year quantities (backtest.by_year already carries random_matched_avg_net_return per year; the path map) are declared 'descriptive, non-decision'. The stated information value ('either way the repo pins the mechanism') is therefore overstated.
- MATCHED-CONTROL ADEQUACY FOR DELAYED ENTRIES. controls.matched_draws matches at the signal session s (features at s; momentum is a broad top tercile of the 20-session return, not an extreme) and _baseline executes the controls under the same ExecConfig from s+k. By s+6 the flagged names have systematically faded (E2: median 5-session return about -10% vs 0% matched in both markets) while controls drifted randomly, so the two populations differ in a known way at the entry session (recent 5-session decline, realized volatility, drawdown). A positive delayed cell vs matched-at-s controls cannot separate 'retained information after an unwind' from a generic short-term reversal after a sharp 5-session decline, a third mechanism the proposal's unwind-vs-lottery dichotomy omits. Matching is also loose on crypto to begin with: in the recorded E2 crypto 2023 run only 30 of 270 attempts matched on all seven features, 16 on price band alone, drawdown was matched for 14% and listing_age for 11% of attempts. Fixable by a pre-registered second control arm re-matched at s+k-1 (same features plus the s..s+k-1 return), not by the current design.
- US ARM: 'PANEL' UNITS EMBED FUTURE REVERSE SPLITS IN THE PRICE LEVELS THAT DRIVE FILLS AND MATCHING. On the repair panel's discovery G2 flags (3,460 flags, 1,224 names): 61% have unit_factor != 1, 43% have a later reverse split of >=10x, 24% >=100x; 10.1% have a panel close above $5,000 and are SKIP_LOT at the $5,000 notional, a skip determined by a reverse split that happens after the trade; 55% of flags sit in a different price/cost band in panel units than as traded, and the matched controls are drawn from that wrong band. research/E2/diagnostics/us_reverse_splits.json shows on 2023 that every SKIP_LOT (154 of 154) was a later-reverse-split name and that such names averaged -6.46% vs -0.33% for the rest: the SKIP_LOT filter removes the worst-outcome population using future information, an outcome-correlated exclusion of the same kind the proposal cites repair_v3 to avoid (quarantine). The pre-registered +1.5%/side stress covers cost-tier error only, not SKIP_LOT or band matching. Direction is flattering for longs, so a US discovery pass could be this artifact.
- MULTIPLICITY AND CONSUMED VALIDATION (declared, but weak guards). 14 selectable, nested, highly correlated cells per market, 28 in all, each at p<0.05 with a 0.005 floor from 200 draws; the per-year rule is no null guard in bull years (2020H2, 2021 crypto: nearly any long is positive) and a market-regime filter in bear years. The single 2023 run per market cannot control the family-wise error of 14 selections, and 2023 has been consumed twice on the same 312 crypto flags (E2 lag1/h5 long, 264 scored; E4 v2 BEAR short subset, 34 scored) and at least 12 times on US; the proposal's own design reasoning cites 2023 numbers (E2 crypto -1.90% vs +1.42%, E4 v2 -1.50%) to reject the regime-gated alternative. The lag-1/h10 and lag-1/h20 cells, which are selectable, include the t+1..t+5 window whose 2023 outcome is known (a known handicap, not a flattering leak).
- CLAIMS THAT CHECK OUT (not refutation): crypto open equals the prior close (median |open/prev-1| = 0.045%, 99th pct 0.73% on pre-2023 rows); sample size on crypto 2023 is not a problem: from the recorded 2023 signal dates alone (312 signals, timing only, no outcomes) every cell has 238-270 attempts and 199-264 trades that can complete inside 2023 (lag11/h20: 238 attempted, <=199 scored), so the stated risk of falling below 100 is overstated; the code claims are accurate (ExecConfig.plan maps a fixed entry's lag to entry_window; execute enters a fixed plan at s+entry_lag ignoring entry_window, so the proposed resolve_plan change is required and sufficient; overlap-skip uses s+entry_lag; e3.harness_config hard-codes entry_lag=1); E3 never delayed an unconditional entry beyond s+1 or held beyond 5; the E4 branch's GLOBAL_HOLDOUT and jump_ratio exist as described. One implementation caveat: the E2 crypto run's recorded config carries units/fill_model/quarantine = None, which execute() rejects if passed through verbatim, so the reproduce stage must coerce those to defaults (how the E4 branch achieved REPRODUCED was not traced).
## fix
Protocol changes that rescue the design (pre-registered before any statistic): (1) adopt E4 v2's jump exclusion on the long side, jump_ratio=10 (EXCLUDED_DATA_JUMP for rule and baselines alike, already in PR #7's execution.py), and record the excluded (ticker, session) list in the discovery output; (2) add a pre-registered tail-robustness co-criterion to admissibility (e.g. the cell must stay positive and p<0.05 after removing its top 1% of trades, or on a 1%-winsorized mean) so selection is not decided by one squeeze; (3) separate the two verdicts: trading admissibility (E3 rule) and the mechanism verdict on rule-minus-matched by year (by_year.random_matched_avg_net_return is already emitted), with the falsifier restated as 'rule minus matched <= 0 at lag 6 and 11 in every discovery year'; (4) add a second control arm re-matched at the entry session (features at s+k-1 plus the s..s+k-1 return) as the mechanism comparison, keeping the harness's matched-at-s arm for comparability; (5) for US, use units='as_traded' for sizing, cost tiers and the matching band (unit_factor exists on the repair panel) or drop the whole-share lot constraint so SKIP_LOT cannot depend on a future reverse split, keep the +1.5%/side stress, and report the share of flags whose status differs between panel and as-traded units; (6) consider p<0.01 on discovery given 28 correlated cells, and state explicitly that a US pass is a replay-year result. Also coerce None config fields to defaults in the reproduce stage.
