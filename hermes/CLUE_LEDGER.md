## Inherited from the Claude cloud era (to 2026-11-02; source: handover 01_EXPERIMENTS_WHAT_WORKED_AND_WHY.md)

- FAIL · shorting spikes (E4, US dump shorts) · CLUE: wins often, but rare squeezes are ruinous; most of the drop happens overnight before a short can open.
- FAIL · early warning from daily price/volume/order flow (E5–E8, LEAD_LADDER, FLOW_IDX) · CLUE: these features predict that a stock will move a lot, not which way.
- FAIL · buying after news and filings (E10, insider buys, 8-K, 13D, Binance 1-min) · CLUE: fast news is priced before you can buy; slow filings are weak. US insider buys came closest (t 2.87).
- FAIL · index rebalancing, futures carry, VIX, classic factors · CLUE: published edges fade; one period carried the results.
- FAIL · BTC+ETH trend account · CLUE: trend rules cut crashes but did not beat holding.
- SMALL WIN · detection: flagged stocks/coins are 4.6–9.3× likelier to hit +30% · CLUE: spotting movers is real; buying them is the problem.
- SMALL WIN · the next-day drop forecaster for US +30% stocks ranks well (score 0.61–0.64) · CLUE: real skill, but shorting it failed on costs and gaps.
- SMALL WIN · share-dilution filings: those stocks did 2.8% worse over 20 days (US) · CLUE: useful as an "avoid" flag.
- UNCLEAR · Binance futures-launch seconds, IDX insider news, MSCI deletions, ETH trend · passed once, failed or unconfirmed later · CLUE: one historical pass is only a hypothesis.
- CLUE · case studies (40 moonshots): only about 1 in 10 had any public warning 3 days ahead, mostly scheduled dates · CLUE: the catalyst is usually same-day; you must be fast, not early.
