# CASES_GLOBAL_v1 report: twenty moonshots outside Indonesia, with SEC filings and news aligned

Descriptive case study. Protocol and selection committed before any case was drawn; 10 US, 5 crypto, 5 from the eToro
exchanges (AU, FR, HK, UK x2), drawn at random from the 2020-07..2022-12 fast-rise episodes. SEC filings for US issuers
from the archived EDGAR submissions; news from four independent researchers, every item with URL and date (`news/`).

## Result

| Class | US (10) | Crypto (5) | Other markets (5) | All (20) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Telegraphed: something public by J-3 | 2 | 1 | 1 | **4** |
| News shock: explanation appears J-2 to J | 5 | 2 | 1 | 8 |
| Price moved before the news | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| Unexplained | 3 | 1 | 3 | 7 |

Compared with Indonesia (CASES_IDX_v1): 0 of 20 telegraphed and 13 of 20 unexplained there; here 12 of 20 have a news
explanation and 4 had something public three sessions out.

## The four early cases, and what they tell an indicator

- **DOCS** (Doximity): earnings date announced three weeks ahead; the results beat.
- **HCM** (HUTCHMED): Hong Kong listing date public a week ahead; the debut traded ~50% above the offer.
- **IMM** (Immutep): trial-data presentation date public seven weeks ahead; the data were positive.
- **OGN** (Origin Protocol): a proposal to airdrop a new token to OGN holders, public on J-3, while BTC and ETH were flat.

Three of the four are **scheduled events**: the date was knowable, the outcome was not. They tell you *when* a large
move may come, not *which way*. Only OGN carried a directional public tell (value promised to holders).

## Same-day news: explains, does not predict

8 cases were explained by news on the day: four company items (a release, trial results, an 8-K, an FDA designation)
and four market-wide events (China policy pledge, Tesla's bitcoin purchase, the Fed, the Pfizer vaccine). The US SEC
filings in the window were mostly routine (Form 4s, quarterly reports, offerings); the explaining 8-K, where there was
one, came on the day. This matches E9: about a quarter of US +30% days carry a filing before the next open, and the
filing arrives with the move.

## Social media and options flow

No dated X, Reddit or StockTwits post surfaced in any search. Unusual Whales data and X are not reachable from this
environment (no connector; both need paid access); options flow is untested here.

## For the indicator

1. **When**: a calendar of scheduled catalysts (earnings dates, trial readouts and medical meetings, listings,
   governance votes and airdrops) marks the days a large move is likely. Combined with tests 28-29 ("hot" names move
   more, both ways), it predicts the size and timing of moves, not their direction.
2. **Which way**: nothing public found here does it, except holder-favourable announcements like OGN's airdrop. Options
   flow before scheduled events is the remaining candidate input.
3. **Indonesia** is different: its moonshots are mostly unexplained thin-stock moves, where broker-level flow is the
   remaining candidate.

20 cases per study: each share is known to about plus or minus 20 points.
