# Interpretation note on the E6 discovery result (added 2026-10-15, after the result; RESULT.md unchanged)

1. **Scope of the interval.** P1's 95% interval for five-day excess, −1.01% to +1.93%, concerns P1's frozen
   rule on its own flag population: 776 flags, Binance spot USDT coins, July 2020 to December 2022. An
   earlier review comment described this as a general ceiling of about 2% on what technical prediction can
   detect. That was wrong and is withdrawn. Another rule, population, horizon or market has its own noise
   and sample size, and E6 says nothing about them.
2. **Placebo limitation, found in review.** Scrambling flow signs left the placebo absorption score defined
   for only 9.5% of P1's flags, while the real score was defined for 99% of placebo flags. The placebo arm was
   therefore drawn from a different, smaller population, and "P1 minus placebo" is a weak test of pressure
   versus volume. P1 failed against matched coins independently, so the gate verdict is unchanged.
3. **Shortened windows.** The four windows that ended early (SRM, COCOS, VIDT, MIR) move P1's excess from
   +0.369% to +0.393% and B0's from −0.595% to −0.582% when dropped. Immaterial; kept as frozen.
