# Dated correction to initial distinctness decision — 16 November 2026

The initial `distinctness.md` is retained unchanged. Its closure of Phase 1 based on the first 20 numeric CIKs was premature. That old-issuer sample was narrow and its zeros could not establish whether a wider, preselected source population had usable facts. The independent review was right to require the bounded corrective census already within the authorized feasibility work.

The corrective roster was fixed by SHA-256 ordering of the remaining frozen E10 issuer identifiers before its sources were read. No replacements, price outcomes, changed window or EPS/EBITDA substitution were used. `source-census-v2-selection.md` records the rule. `census-v2-first20.json` and `census-v2-last20.json` preserve the observations and unknowns.

## What changes the decision

Across the 40 selected issuers, the two agents observed 32 earnings releases. There are **two explicit as-reported source pairs**: Kratos and Ribbon. Adeia is a third, complete-component derivation, recorded separately rather than added to the predeclared explicit count. Eight issuer calendars/event inventories remain incomplete or outside the window on the bounded evidence. Oracle also has an investor-day disclosure; it is not silently counted as an earnings release.

Thus the observed explicit source-pair discovery fraction is 2/40 selected issuers, or 2/32 observed earnings releases, in this one window and this historically selected E10 population. These are not certified admission rates. Kratos has an intervening acquisition/perimeter issue; Ribbon's latest-prior completeness is not fully established. Both are useful extraction cases, even though forecast margins deteriorated. None is evidence of trading profit. Do not extrapolate these small, source-bounded counts into a precise annual arrival rate or evidence-completion date.

Ribbon's fact is explicitly present inside a non-GAAP reconciliation as a GAAP operating-loss row. Rejecting the release merely because its headline mentions EBITDA would miss it. Adeia's complete expense identity is a defensible extraction extension if declared in advance. These cases show a concrete role for contextual reading and accounting checks, but do not prove that an LLM improves on a competent conventional extractor.

## Revised recommendation

**Proceed with a narrowly scoped, reviewable fact contract and offline extraction fixtures; do not treat the original zeros as a reason to close the lead.** The candidate remains economically distinct from the inspected revenue-only E10 rule: same-year operating-margin revision alongside the revenue revision. Keep negative, unchanged, incompatible and unknown cases in the development set. Reuse existing KOD ingestion and ledger foundations.

This is not an unconditional go for the complete deployed trading study. Before a recurring experiment starts, its protocol needs a specific prospective source population, latest-comparable-prior rule, accounting/perimeter rules, actual arrival/completion clocks, provider/usage configuration and a credible evidence-duration calculation with uncertainty. Those are separate readiness questions. A nonexistent historical KOD receipt timestamp is **not** a fatal objection to prospective research: record the real future timestamp and classify these historical examples as development material.

Do not expand the economic hypothesis after seeing these cases. Keep the comparison on the same event set: the existing revenue fact versus revenue plus the specified operating-margin fact. If LLM value is claimed, compare it against a non-LLM implementation given the same source/fact information; otherwise the experiment measures the added fact, not the model. A later failure to improve after-cost, feasible-entry account value rejects the trading hypothesis even if extraction works perfectly.

The prior-test map in `distinctness.md` still stands, including the unresolved exact CONTRACT_MIN source.
